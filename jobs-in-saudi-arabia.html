Stay safe when applying for jobs abroad

  • Read the full contract before you sign. Check salary, hours, housing and contract length.
  • Ask for every fee in writing and pay only to official company accounts.
  • Keep copies of all receipts and documents.
  • Never hand over your passport to anyone who is not an official.
