Stay safe. Avoid fake agents.

Overseas job scams are common. Check everything before you pay anyone, including us.

Before you pay

  • Ask for every fee in writing
  • Pay only to official company accounts
  • Keep copies of receipts

Before you sign

  • Read the full contract
  • Check salary, hours and housing
  • Never hand over your passport to anyone but officials
